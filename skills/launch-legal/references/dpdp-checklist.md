## Statutory Requirements Checklist

### 1. Grounds for Processing
- [ ] **Lawful Purpose:** Personal data is processed solely for a lawful purpose for which consent has been obtained or for specified legitimate uses under Section 7.
  - *Citation:* Section 4(1)(a)-(b) & Section 4(2).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=4)

### 2. Itemised Notice Before Consent
- [ ] **Accompanying / Preceding Notice:** Every consent request is accompanied or preceded by a notice informing the Data Principal of:
  1. The specific personal data collected and the exact purpose for processing.
  2. The manner in which the Data Principal may withdraw consent (Section 6(4)) and seek grievance redressal (Section 13).
  3. The manner in which a complaint may be made to the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI).
  - *Citation:* Section 5(1)(i)-(iii).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=4)
- [ ] **Notice for Pre-existing Data:** For consent obtained before Act commencement, notice is provided as soon as reasonably practicable via email, in-app notification, or other effective method.
  - *Citation:* Section 5(2)(a)-(b).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=5)
- [ ] **Multilingual Access:** The Data Principal is given the option to access the notice in English or any of the 22 languages specified in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution of India.
  - *Citation:* Section 5(3).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=5)

### 3. Consent Architecture & Unbundling
- [ ] **Free, Specific, Informed, Unconditional & Unambiguous:** Consent must be indicated through a clear affirmative action (e.g., unticked checkbox; pre-checked boxes are prohibited).
  - *Citation:* Section 6(1).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=5)
- [ ] **Strict Purpose Limitation (No Bundling):** Consent is limited strictly to personal data necessary for the specified service. Unrelated permissions (e.g., phone contact list access for an e-commerce or telemedicine app) cannot be bundled or conditioned as a prerequisite for service delivery.
  - *Citation:* Section 6(1) Illustration.
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=5)
- [ ] **Plain Language & Contact Point:** Consent requests are presented in clear, plain language with the option of English or Eighth Schedule languages, and provide the contact details of a Data Protection Officer (DPO) or authorized respondent.
  - *Citation:* Section 6(3).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=6)
- [ ] **Comparable Ease of Withdrawal:** The Data Principal can withdraw consent at any time. The technical ease of withdrawing consent must be comparable to the ease with which consent was given (e.g., 1-click setting if signup was 1-click).
  - *Citation:* Section 6(4).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=6)

### 4. Obligations of Data Fiduciary & Processors
- [ ] **Fiduciary Accountability:** The Data Fiduciary remains legally responsible for compliance irrespective of any agreement to the contrary or processor failures.
  - *Citation:* Section 8(1).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=6)
- [ ] **Valid Processor Contracts:** Third-party processors (cloud hosting, email delivery, analytics, payment gateways) are engaged only under a valid, written contract.
  - *Citation:* Section 8(2).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=7)
- [ ] **Data Accuracy:** Personal data used to make decisions affecting the user or disclosed to another fiduciary is maintained with completeness, accuracy, and consistency.
  - *Citation:* Section 8(3).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=7)
- [ ] **Reasonable Security Safeguards:** Implement technical and organizational measures to prevent personal data breaches (encryption in transit and rest, role-based access control, monitoring).
  - *Citation:* Section 8(4)-(5).
  - *Statutory Penalty:* Up to ₹250 crore (Section 33 & Schedule Item 1).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=7,page=21)
- [ ] **Mandatory Breach Notification:** In the event of a personal data breach, immediate intimation must be provided to both:
  1. The Data Protection Board of India (DPBI), and
  2. Each affected Data Principal.
  - *Citation:* Section 8(6).
  - *Statutory Penalty:* Up to ₹200 crore (Section 33 & Schedule Item 2).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=7,page=21)
- [ ] **Storage Limitation & Deletion:** Personal data must be erased when consent is withdrawn or as soon as it is reasonable to assume the specified purpose is no longer served (unless retention is required by another applicable law). Processors must be directed to erase all copies.
  - *Citation:* Section 8(7)-(8).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=7)
- [ ] **Published Contact Details:** Publish the business contact information of a Data Protection Officer or an authorized representative who can answer user queries regarding data processing.
  - *Citation:* Section 8(9).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=8)
- [ ] **Grievance Redressal Mechanism:** Provide readily available grievance redressal channels with defined resolution timelines.
  - *Citation:* Section 8(10) & Section 13(1)-(2).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=8,page=10)

### 5. Children's Personal Data Protection (< 18 Years)
- [ ] **Verifiable Parental Consent:** Before processing data of a child (individual under 18 years per Section 2(f)), obtain verifiable consent of the parent or lawful guardian.
  - *Citation:* Section 9(1).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=8)
- [ ] **Ban on Detrimental Processing:** No data processing that is likely to cause any detrimental effect on the well-being of a child.
  - *Citation:* Section 9(2).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=8)
- [ ] **Absolute Ban on Tracking & Targeted Advertising:** Absolute prohibition on tracking, behavioral monitoring, or serving targeted advertisements directed at children.
  - *Citation:* Section 9(3).
  - *Statutory Penalty:* Up to ₹200 crore (Section 33 & Schedule Item 3).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=8,page=21)

### 6. Significant Data Fiduciary (SDF) Criteria
*(Applies if notified by Central Government based on data volume, sensitivity, or risk under Section 10)*
- [ ] Appoint an India-based Data Protection Officer responsible directly to the Board of Directors.
- [ ] Appoint an independent data auditor.
- [ ] Conduct periodic Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) and periodic data audits.
  - *Citation:* Section 10(1)-(2).
  - *Statutory Penalty:* Up to ₹150 crore (Section 33 & Schedule Item 4).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=9,page=21)

### 7. Data Principal Statutory Rights
- [ ] **Right to Access Information:** Right to obtain a summary of personal data processed, processing activities, and identities of all other Data Fiduciaries and Processors with whom data has been shared.
  - *Citation:* Section 11(1)(a)-(c).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=10)
- [ ] **Right to Correction and Erasure:** Right to correction of inaccurate data, completion of incomplete data, updating, and erasure of personal data unless required by law.
  - *Citation:* Section 12(1)-(3).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=10)
- [ ] **Right to Nominate:** Facility allowing the Data Principal to nominate an individual to exercise their rights in the event of death or incapacity.
  - *Citation:* Section 14(1)-(2).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=11)

### 8. Cross-Border Transfers
- [ ] **Cross-Border Transfer Rules:** Transfers of personal data outside India are permitted by default, except to countries explicitly restricted/blacklisted by the Central Government, and subject to any stricter sector-specific laws (e.g., RBI payment data localisation).
  - *Citation:* Section 16(1)-(2).
  - *Source:* [MeitY Gazette PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf#page=11)
