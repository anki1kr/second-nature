# Step 4 — E-E-A-T

Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust — as visible page content, not a vibe.

## Checks

1. **First-hand data.** Numbers, timings, or measurements the publisher generated itself (its own runs, its own counts) instead of restating claims from elsewhere. First-hand data is also the strongest AI-citation bait: engines prefer to quote what nobody else has.
2. **Dated facts.** Facts that can go stale carry a "checked on <date>" or "as of <date>" marker in the visible text, not only in a schema date.
3. **Sources linked.** Claims taken from external sources link to them. Unlinked claims ("according to a 2023 study") weaken trust and give engines nothing to verify.
4. **Who made it.** A named author or publisher, visible on the page, linked to a real profile where reasonable. A page with no human or organization behind it loses trust signals on exactly the topics where trust is measured hardest.
