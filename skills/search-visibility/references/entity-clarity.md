# Step 3 — Entity clarity

Search and answer engines resolve the page to an entity. Ambiguity about what the page is about splits or blocks that resolution.

## Checks

1. **Full term spelled out once.** Where the subject is known by an acronym that is ambiguous (an acronym shared with another product or concept), the full term appears spelled out at least once on the page, with the acronym next to it.
2. **One consistent name.** The product/topic is called the same thing in the title, the meta description, the H1, and the body. Alternating names ("X Tracker", "X tracker app", "X") split the entity.
3. **Disambiguation from same-name sites.** If other sites/products share the name, the page says what makes it distinct in words an engine can quote (e.g. a sentence naming what it is and who it is for), rather than relying on the domain alone.
